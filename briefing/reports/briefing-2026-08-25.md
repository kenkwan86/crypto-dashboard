## 3. Volatility

BTC DVOL is 44.0, at the 27th percentile of the trailing 1 year. That is low-to-moderate, not elevated, despite the regime score being hot. Vol markets are not pricing much event risk right now.

Term structure is upward-sloping (contango) for both BTC and ETH: near-dated IV is lower than far-dated. BTC ATM IV runs from 45.6 (Aug 29 expiry) up to 42.6 (Jun 2027), a fairly flat curve overall. ETH is similar, 62.0 down to 56.7-56.7 across the curve, also fairly flat with a modest term premium further out.

Skew (RR25, call IV minus put IV) is positive across nearly every BTC and ETH expiry, meaning calls are bid over puts — a call skew. BTC RR25 peaks at 4.31 (Sep 4 expiry) and fades toward flat/negative by mid-2027 (-0.08). ETH RR25 is much larger, peaking at 7.4 (Sep 4 expiry) and staying positive out to mid-2027 (1.30). This confirms the options market is leaning bullish and paying up for upside convexity, consistent with the strong momentum and OI buildup in the futures data.

## 5. Watchlist

1. **BTC/ETH funding turning positive and rising** — funding z90 is still low (0.12 / 0.88). If it climbs toward 2+ while OI stays this elevated, the setup shifts from "building" to "crowded and vulnerable."
2. **OI z90 rolling over on BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP, ZEC** — any of these dropping from the 8-9 range back toward 3-4 would signal deleveraging, likely alongside a price pullback.
3. **DVOL breaking above ~50 (roughly the 1-year 60th-70th percentile zone)** — a jump from the current 27th percentile would show vol markets catching up to the hot positioning.
4. **RR25 collapsing toward zero or negative on near-dated BTC/ETH expiries** — would signal put demand overtaking call demand, an early hedging or reversal signal.
5. **Regime score continuing above 1.28 vs. stalling** — a push toward 2+ would confirm the hot phase is extending; a reversal back under 0.5 next week would suggest last week's move was a spike, not a trend.
