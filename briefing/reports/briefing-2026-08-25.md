# Market Regime Briefing — 2026-08-25

## 1. Regime assessment
The regime score is 0.84, the highest reading in the 90-day window shown. The score was negative for most of June and July (as low as -0.81 on 2026-06-04), turned positive on 2026-08-13 at 0.34, and has climbed since. The trend is up and the market is now hot, not neutral. The main drivers are OI z-score at 2.38 and momentum z-score at 1.67. Funding is not stretched: funding z is -0.03 and funding percentile is -0.92, so this is a leverage-and-trend-driven heat-up, not a funding-driven one.

## 2. Positioning extremes
OI is the standout stretch across the board:
- TRUMP: OI up 151% in 7 days, OI z90 = 3.61, momentum z90 = 3.55. The most extreme combination in the set.
- PROM: OI up 231% in 7 days, OI z90 = 3.16.

- BTC: OI z90 = 2.82, OI up 23% in 7 days, ranked 4th-largest OI. Funding z90 is negative (-0.87), so BTC's OI surge is not funding-chased longs — more likely spot-driven or hedging flow.
- ETH: OI z90 = 2.63, OI up 30% in 7 days, funding z90 near flat (-0.19).

Funding itself is not extreme anywhere in the top-cap names. The most negative funding z-scores are STORJ (-8.10) and SUPER (-5.68), both small-cap and likely idiosyncratic (STORJ funding rate is -1.51%, an outlier print). No large-cap coin shows crowded-long funding, which means this OI build is not yet paired with expensive long funding — a classic mid-cycle, not late-cycle, signature.

Implication: positioning is stretched on size (OI), not on cost (funding). That is consistent with fresh directional conviction rather than a crowded, expensive long that's vulnerable to a funding squeeze.

## 3. Volatility
BTC DVOL is 44.0, at the 58th percentile of the past year — moderate, not cheap and not extreme. The term structure is in backwardation at the front and flattens further out: front-week ATM IV is ~50-51%, falling to ~41-42% by the September 25 expiry and staying near 42% out to March 2027. That front-end premium says the market is pricing near-term event or realized-vol risk higher than the medium term.

Skew (RR25, call IV minus put IV) is positive and steepest at the front: +3.48 to +4.31 for expiries inside 10 days, decaying to +0.39 by December and going slightly negative (-0.08) by June 2027. Positive front-end skew means calls are bid over puts — the options market is leaning bullish on near-term upside, consistent with the OI-driven momentum picture in the futures data. ETH skew is even more pronounced: RR25 of +6.14 to +7.40 in the front month, easing to +1.3 further out.

## 4. Liquidation picture
There was a large short-liquidation flush on 2026-08-19 ($801.6M short-side) followed by elevated short liquidations on 2026-08-20 ($219.4M) and 2026-08-21 ($477.7M). This is a squeeze pattern: shorts got forced out over three days, aligning with the regime score's turn positive on 2026-08-13 and its acceleration since. After that, liquidations rebalanced — 2026-08-22 flipped to long-heavy ($257.6M long vs $111.2M short), and the last three days (08-23 to 08-25) show moderate, roughly two-sided flushes ($75-155M per side), suggesting the initial short squeeze has worked through and the market is now digesting the move rather than continuing to force positions out.

## 5. Watchlist
1. **TRUMP and PROM OI**: both show 7-day OI growth above 150%, the most extreme in the set. A reversal in price with OI still elevated would signal late-stage crowding — watch for OI z90 to roll over from >3.
2. **Funding turning positive on BTC/ETH**: currently near flat to negative despite the OI surge. If funding z90 flips positive and rises alongside continued OI growth, that shifts this from "size-driven" to "cost-driven" crowding — a more fragile setup.
3. **DVOL percentile**: at 58%, still room to run before "extreme." A push above the 80th percentile alongside a regime score near 1.0+ would flag genuine euphoria.
4. **RR25 front-end**: currently +3.5 to +4.3 (BTC) and +6-7 (ETH). A sharp compression toward zero or negative would mean the options market is pricing downside protection again — an early warning the bullish lean is fading.
5. **Renewed large one-sided liquidations**: the 08-19 to 08-21 short squeeze has quieted into two-sided flushes. A fresh spike in long liquidations above the recent ~$150-260M range would mark the next leg of unwind, not continuation.

**Data gap**: oi_change_7d and oi_z90 are null for most altcoins outside the top ~10 by OI (LINK, BCH, SUI, ADA, and most others), so the positioning-extremes section above is limited to names with populated OI history — broader breadth in OI stretch outside majors can't be assessed here.
